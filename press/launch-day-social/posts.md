## LinkedIn

**Image:** linkedin-image

**Post:**

Today I'm launching my first iPhone app on Product Hunt: Automono.

As a designer, I've shot black and white on a Fujifilm X100F for years. I love what it does to a photo: it strips away the noise and leaves light, shape and feeling. But on my iPhone, every black and white app asked me to change how I shoot, with a new camera app, or editing photos one by one afterwards.

So I built the thing I wanted.

Automono works with the iPhone's own Camera. You shoot in color as usual, and it develops a black and white print beside every photo, automatically.

A few decisions I'm proud of:

→ No new habits. You never need to open the app. A one-minute Shortcuts setup lets it run in the background.
→ Real quality. Prints are developed from RAW when you shoot ProRAW, saved as full-res 10-bit files.
→ Additive only. It never edits or deletes an original.
→ Private by design. Everything happens on the device: no account, no uploads, no analytics.

It's free on the App Store, and it's live on Product Hunt today:
https://www.producthunt.com/posts/automono

If you have a minute, I'd really value your feedback in the comments there. And if you know someone who loves black and white photography, please send this their way.
